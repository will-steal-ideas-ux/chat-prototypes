Summarize the Q3 financial report — key numbers and what changed versus last year.
Claude Opus 4.8

Q3 was a strong quarter overall. Revenue reached €48.2M, up 14% year over year, with the strongest gains in EMEA. Net margin improved to 18%, and OpEx held essentially flat all year despite the headcount additions in engineering.

What drove the quarter

  • EMEA expansion was the largest single contributor, led by two enterprise renewals and a new German partner that closed mid-quarter.
  • Net margin of 18% reflects structural savings in cloud spend and a more favorable product mix toward higher-tier plans.
  • OpEx discipline kept operating costs flat even as the team grew, improving operating leverage going into Q4.

Looking ahead, the pipeline supports continued double-digit growth, though renewal timing in the Americas remains the main variable to watch heading into year-end.
