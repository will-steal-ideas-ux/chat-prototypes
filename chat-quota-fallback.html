Executive Summary: Cybersecurity Market Opportunity

The global cybersecurity market represents one of the most structurally durable growth opportunities in enterprise technology. With spend projected to exceed $298 billion by 2028 (13.4% CAGR), the sector is being propelled by three interlocking forces: the weaponization of AI by threat actors, the accelerating migration of critical infrastructure to cloud environments, and tightening regulatory requirements across North America, the EU, and APAC.

Market leadership has consolidated around platform players — CrowdStrike's Falcon platform and Palo Alto Networks' Cortex suite — who are successfully monetizing the enterprise shift from point solutions toward unified security operations. Meanwhile, cloud-native challengers like Wiz are capturing disproportionate wallet-share in the fastest-growing segment.

The near-term catalyst is the AI threat surface: 73% of CISOs surveyed in Q1 2026 cited AI-augmented phishing and automated vulnerability exploitation as their top-of-mind concerns, driving budget reallocation toward detection and response tooling at the expense of perimeter-first investments.
